# Questions to ask the interviewer

A running bank of questions to draw on at the end of an interview. Keep the ones
that consistently land; add company-specific questions per application.

## Role & team

- What would the first 90 days look like — what would "going well" mean?
- How is the team structured, and who would I work with most closely?
- What are the biggest challenges the team is facing right now?
- Why is this position open?

## Engineering practice

- What does the path from commit to production look like?
- How do you balance shipping features against paying down technical debt?
- How are technical decisions made, and disagreements resolved?
- What does on-call / incident response look like?

## Product & direction

- What are the most important goals for the product over the next year?
- How does the team decide what to build next?
- How close does engineering get to the users?

## Growth

- How do you support engineers growing into more senior or lead roles?
- What have people who did well in this role gone on to do?
- How is feedback given, and how often?

## Logistics

- What are the next steps in the process, and the timeline?
- What is the expectation around working hours and remote / office?
